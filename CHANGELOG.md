# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-06

First version. Implements MDZip spec 1.2 and passes the spec's shared
fixtures from [v1.2.1](https://github.com/mdzip-project/mdzip-spec/releases/tag/v1.2.1),
including the T-018 ZIP-bomb tests. Pure Lua 5.3+, no dependencies.

- Reading: `mdzip.open` (bytes) and `open_file`, `entries`, `files`,
  `directories`, `documents`, `special_entries`, `find`, `has`, `read`,
  `read_text`, `manifest`, `spec_version`, `entry_point`, `mode`, `title`.
- Links: `resolve_link` inside the archive (spec 9.3). Links out of or into
  archives come back unresolved.
- Validation: `validate()` / `mdzip.validate(bytes)`, with spec error codes
  and `WARN_*` warnings.
- Writing: `mdzip.create` / `create_file`, and `archive:update` /
  `mdzip.update_file`, which copies untouched entries as stored, keeps unknown
  manifest fields in order, keeps symbolic links with a warning, and refuses
  changes that would move or break the entry point.
- Safety: rejects unsafe, duplicate and conflicting paths, encrypted entries,
  compression other than stored/DEFLATE, overlapping entries; never follows
  symbolic links; built-in limits on entry count, sizes and compression ratio,
  checked at open and again while decompressing.
- Built in: ZIP reading/writing, DEFLATE, CRC-32, strict JSON, and Unicode NFC
  and case folding (data from Unicode 16.0, generated by `tools/gen_unicode.py`).
